Mirror holder

A wedge for a flat mirror, with a captive 1/4" nut in its base for a Picatinny camera mount. Adjust it to your mirror and download the STL.

A do-it-yourself project. No product, no warranty: you print, build and use it at your own risk.

The face is a little flatter than 45° (now 43.5°), so a view turned by the mirror rises 3° above the axis; set the tilt to 0 for exactly 90°. Glue the mirror flat onto the face, centred: its centre sits about 18 mm behind the front edge and 29 mm above the base. Prints as it stands, without supports. If your camera mount has a threaded hole instead of a screw, choose the version with a screw.

Your browser can’t show the 3D preview (WebGL is off). The download works anyway.
