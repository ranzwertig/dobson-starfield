III. Zenigo Pro

  1. What Pro includes. The list on zenigo.app/pro and the descriptions in the help at the time of purchase apply. Features marked there as coming later are not part of what you buy. At the time of writing, Pro includes:
    1. Sync of the observing list, logbook and settings between your devices, end-to-end encrypted.
    2. Coming up: eclipses, close approaches of the Moon and planets, and meteor showers.
    3. Satellites: the ISS and bright satellites on the map, and their next visible passes.
    4. Eyepiece view of the target as it appears in your eyepiece, and the best of your eyepieces for each object.
    5. Short route through your planned objects.
    6. Live connection: pairs two devices by QR code; the leading device sends where the telescope points and the way to the target, and optionally its camera image; the following device shows them. The devices connect directly over a shared Wi-Fi network or a phone’s hotspot, without our servers.
    7. Extended catalogue: about 10,000 more deep-sky objects of the NGC and IC catalogues and about 790,000 more stars down to magnitude 11, in addition to the free catalogue.
    8. Observing programmes: Binocular Messier, Caldwell, Herschel 400, Herschel II and the Saguaro Astronomy Club’s 110 Best of the NGC, with progress from your logbook, planning for the night and the programme’s logbook as CSV or a page to print. The Messier programme is free.
    9. Several instruments: more than one telescope or binoculars, each with its own settings, and switching between them. One instrument with any number of eyepieces is free.
    Planned and marked as coming: logbook statistics.
  2. Requirements. Pro needs a current version of Safari, Chrome or Edge. Sync, satellite data and the one-time download of the extended catalogue (about 6 MB per device) need an internet connection; everything else in Pro works offline. The live connection needs both devices to reach each other on the same network; some networks block direct connections between devices, for example guest networks, and the connection then does not work there.
  3. Which device needs Pro. Pro features work on each device on which you enter the key. For the live connection, only the leading device needs Pro; any device can follow it without a licence, including devices of other people, for example at a public observing night.

The licence is personal: you may enter the key on all devices you own or use yourself. It may not be shared with other people or used by an organisation for several people at the same time.

When the licence period ends, the Pro features stop working. The free features and your data on your devices remain; logbook entries for objects of the extended catalogue stay readable. Zenigo reminds you 30 days before the end.

  7. Licence check. Zenigo checks the key and its end date on your device. No online activation and no account are needed, so the offline Pro features also work in an installed copy without a connection until the licence period ends.
