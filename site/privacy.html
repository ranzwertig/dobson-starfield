What the app processes on your device

Camera and photos

When you start the camera or choose a photo, the image is analysed in your browser to find the stars in it. Images are not stored and not uploaded. The camera only runs while you have it switched on, and only after you have allowed access.

Location

The app needs your approximate position to convert star positions into directions for your telescope. You can enter it by hand or let the browser determine it (the “GPS” button in Settings), which asks for your permission first. The position is only stored on your device.

Motion sensors and compass

If you turn on “Align”, the app reads the orientation sensors of your device to move the map with the phone. This data is processed in the moment and not stored, except for a compass correction you choose to save.

Settings stored on your device

Your settings (location, camera field of view, eyepiece point, compass correction, map filter, chosen target and display options) are saved in your browser’s local storage, so they are still there next time. For offline use the browser also keeps a copy of the app (service worker cache). This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the website data for Zenigo in your browser settings.

No cookies, no tracking

Zenigo sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and has no social media plugins. Fonts are delivered from our own server, not from third-party providers.

Links to other websites

The object details in the app link to external sources (SEDS Messier Database, In-The-Sky.org, NASA, SIMBAD and Wikipedia), and the menu links to weather forecasts (meteoblue, Clear Outside). The forecast links contain the coordinates of your location, rounded to about 100 m, so the provider can show the forecast for it. These sites are only contacted when you tap a link. From then on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and the content of your message only to answer your inquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your inquiry concerns a contract. We delete the messages when they are no longer needed, unless legal retention periods apply.
