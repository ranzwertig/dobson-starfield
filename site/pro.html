Questions

Is it a subscription?
No. You pay once for 12 months. There is nothing to cancel, and nothing is charged again unless you buy again.
On how many devices can I use it?
On all devices you use yourself. For the live connection, only the device on the telescope needs Pro; others can follow it, also at a public observing night.
Do I need an account?
No. You get a licence key that Zenigo checks on the device. Zenigo stores no email address; Paddle needs one for the receipt.
What if I delete Zenigo or change phones?
Your key comes back from a backup, from the email you can send yourself after buying, or with your order reference; otherwise write to us. See Lost licence key.
